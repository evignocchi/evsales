# Surface: evsales.it home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: owner of a small Italian SME arriving from Enea's cold email or phone/WhatsApp contact, checking who he is. Job: understand in seconds who Enea is, what he does and how; act: fill the 3-question form and book the free 45 minutes on Cal.com. Proof: Enea's real story and photo, a visible method, an illustrated case labelled "una PMI tipo" with fictional data. Constraints: Italian, "io", no invented clients/numbers, white ground, dark ink, one accent only for the CTA, no stock photos.

User steer: chose the glazier-partition challenger, "stile Apple".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a steel-and-glass office partition (the "vetrata" of the owner's office), built with Apple product-page restraint. Frosted panes hide the chaos of scattered contacts; as you scroll the glass clears and the chaos becomes an ordered pipeline. Refuses the split hero with portrait plus badge row and the centred headline over equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: White and #F5F5F7 panes, anthracite steel mullions (3px, square joints) as the only line ink, Geist self-hosted, huge tight display, generous air. Cobalt glass is the single saturated pane: CTA only. Frost (backdrop blur) means "not clear yet"; clear glass means "in order". Hover backlights a pane under the pointer.

STORY: I lose contacts; this person is a salesperson who builds; process first, tool second; I can see what I'd get; 45 free minutes give me my 3 leaks; quote before starting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width crittall wall. Largest pane: headline "I tuoi contatti sono dappertutto." plus subline. Right column: portrait pane (dark photo) above cobalt CTA pane holding the first form question as live chips. Lower panes frosted over WhatsApp/Excel/post-it fragments.

FORM: Glazier colour-field partition (challenger craft-making-glazier-colorfield-partition), fused with Apple product-page pacing. Seed key 7b89a814 (re-roll 1).

Signature interaction: glass clears on scroll (blur 18px to 0) while fragments fly into pipeline columns. Motion grammar: slow brightening and unblurring, nothing slides fast, exponential ease-out.
