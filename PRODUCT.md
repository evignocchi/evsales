# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, deployed on Cloudflare Pages from this repository. Domain: evsales.it. Booking via Cal.com embed (account/event slug still to be provided).

## Users

Owners ("titolari") of small Italian SMEs, typically 5 to 30 people with a small sales team or a few agents. Their contacts live in WhatsApp, Excel and the salespeople's heads; nobody knows for sure who to call back. They reach the site after receiving a cold email or a phone/WhatsApp contact from Enea, and visit to check who he is before replying or booking. They are not CRM experts and do not use CRM jargon.

## Product Purpose

evsales.it is Enea Vignocchi's personal consulting brand. He designs the client's sales process and then puts it into a CRM the team actually uses: process first, tool second. The site is a validation page for outbound, not a traffic engine. Success: a visitor understands in seconds who Enea is, what he does and how, trusts him enough to fill the short qualification form and book the free 45-minute call.

## Positioning

A salesperson who has seen CRMs fail from the inside (Sales Executive / Country Launcher, Fractional Head of Sales, CSM) and who also builds (HubSpot, n8n and Claude Code automations, AI-built sites and portals). He designs the sales process before touching any software, keeps the tech simple for small teams, and can stay on afterwards as sales support.

## Operating Context

- Three-step path: 1) design of the sales process, 2) CRM configuration, automations and team training, 3) optional ongoing sales support (a natural consequence, the door to fractional sales).
- Deliverables: process map document (stages, hand-off rules, who does what); CRM configured with contacts imported and cleaned from Excel/address books; basic automations (follow-ups, reminders, lead assignment, notifications); team training sessions plus a short user guide, with 30 days of post go-live support.
- Tool-neutral approach, honestly stating he works mostly with HubSpot.
- Pricing: custom, a fixed-scope quote after the call. No public prices.
- Single CTA everywhere: free 45-minute consultation. Promise: you leave with the 3 main leaks in your sales process. A short form (team size, how contacts are managed today, tools used) precedes booking.
- Outbound channels: cold email and phone/WhatsApp. Messages must be able to link straight to the booking step.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Italian. Voice: first person singular ("io").
- Legal: footer with business data (Partita IVA, to be provided), privacy policy and cookie banner as required in Italy.
- Possible conflicts with current roles (exclusivity clauses) are still to be verified; the site must not be launched publicly before Enea confirms. Naming of current employers is provisional until then.
- Open: Cal.com URL, LinkedIn URL, P.IVA, final privacy/cookie texts.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: evsales.it. No existing logo or colors; to be created.
- Brief-pinned aesthetic: white background, dark colors (navy, anthracite or deep green), a single accent color reserved for the CTA, generous white space, clean typography, no stock photos. Premium but accessible: sober and clear, not luxurious.
- Tone: direct and concrete. Speak with the owner's words ("non perdo più i contatti", "so chi devo richiamare"); name the CRM only after the problem. Automations are framed as "automazioni che fanno il lavoro noioso al posto tuo"; tech simplifies, it never shows off.
- References given by Enea: skmarketing.it, coldiq.com, frontal.so, noon.framer.website, halden-miller.webflow.io, and a "Stride" hero screenshot (large type over a portrait). He asked for a very high-end design with standout features.

- Career facts: Sales Executive / Country Launcher at Enginy.io; Fractional Head of Sales for a software house; CSM at Aiclo; Economics and Business Management degree, Università Cattolica; consulting experience with UpdateGroup (manufacturing); real example of an onboarding portal built for Aiclo.
- Absent and never to be invented: client installations (he has never done a CRM install for a third party), testimonials, client logos, numbers or results from real clients, a publicly declared pilot program.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over polish: never imply experience, clients or results that do not exist.
2. The owner's problem first, the tool after.
3. Simple systems the team will actually use beat sophisticated ones.
4. Clarity in five seconds for someone arriving from an outbound message.
5. One action: book the 45 minutes.
